## Why This Was Built

Behavioral interviews are usually the round people prepare least for and lose on. The questions are
predictable — a conflict, a failure, a time you led something — and the difference between a vague answer
and a compelling one is almost entirely preparation.

I have genuinely good material from years in property management: difficult conversations, competing
priorities, decisions with real consequences for people's housing. STAR is the structure that turns that
experience into an answer with a clear result instead of a rambling anecdote.

## Why This Matters (Industry Application)

Behavioral rounds carry real weight, especially at larger companies, and a career-changer has to make the
prior career an asset rather than a gap to explain. My operations background is a differentiator — but only
if I can tell it well.
